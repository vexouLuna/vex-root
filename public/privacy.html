Privacy policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

vexoul.net and the sites under it (such as vods.vexoul.net and dtp.vexoul.net) are run by vexouLuna ("I"). This page covers what they share: how they are served and the Twitch sign-in. A site that handles more than this says so on its own privacy page.

Visiting

  • The sites set no advertising or analytics cookies.
  • Requests reach the sites through Cloudflare, which processes your IP address to deliver the page.

Signing in with Twitch

Signing in is optional. One sign-in, at auth.vexoul.net, works on every vexoul.net site. A plain sign-in asks Twitch for no extra permissions; an admin page may ask for one more, which Twitch shows you before you agree. The sign-in stores:

  • your Twitch user id, login, display name, avatar and chat colour;
  • the Twitch tokens issued at sign-in, encrypted;
  • your sessions, kept as a hash in an HttpOnly cookie;
  • your VOD watch progress, so it follows you between devices;
  • a log of sign-in events with your IP address.

None of it is sold or shared with anyone.

Deleting your account

"Forget me" on vexoul.net/account revokes the Twitch tokens, deletes your account, every session and your watch progress, and removes your id and IP address from the sign-in log. Signing in again afterwards makes a new, empty account.

It does not delete what a site keeps on its own, such as chat logs kept by the chat bot, the sites' admin logs, or server logs, which keep sign-in lines until they rotate out.
