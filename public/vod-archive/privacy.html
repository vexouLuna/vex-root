Privacy policy

VOD archive · last updated 15 November 2026

This policy covers the VOD archive: the application that records a channel's Twitch broadcasts and uploads them to that channel's YouTube account. It is run by vexouLuna and serves the archive sites that link to this page. It covers the application only. A site that uses it may keep other data, and says so itself.

Who it concerns

Only the owner of a YouTube channel who connects that channel to the archive. People who visit an archive site are never asked to connect a Google account, and the application receives no Google data about them.

Google and YouTube data

The application uses the YouTube API Services.

  • What it accesses: the connected YouTube account, through the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube permission.
  • What it stores: the OAuth access and refresh tokens, the permissions granted, and the channel's id, title and URL. They are kept in the database of the archive that channel belongs to.
  • What it uses them for: uploading that archive's videos to the connected channel, and checking that the channel can still be uploaded to. Nothing else.
  • Sharing: this data is not sold, not shared with or transferred to anyone, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models.
  • Keeping: the tokens are kept for as long as the channel stays connected. Connecting again replaces them.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google's own handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

  • Revoke the application at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The stored tokens stop working at once.
  • To have the stored tokens and channel details deleted as well, email the address below.
